Intekhab

"Intekhab" is an emotional drama of love, greed, sacrifice and broken relationships revolving around two completely different sisters, Muskaan and Mohini. Muskaan is kind-hearted, caring, and believes in relationships, while Mohini is ambitious and obsessed with wealth and luxury. Mohini trapped Mohib, a sincere businessman deeply in love with her, but on the day of their Nikah she suddenly refuses to marry him after knowing about his UK visa rejection. To save the family’s respect, Muskaan faced the family pressure to marry Mohib instead. Mohini moves on to another wealthy man, Zaroon and marries him against her family’s wishes, leading her father to disown her. But Mohini only cares about the money and status. As relationships break and realities unfold, story of “Intekhab” explores whether selfish desires can ever bring happiness, and whether true love and sacrifice can survive betrayal and pain.
